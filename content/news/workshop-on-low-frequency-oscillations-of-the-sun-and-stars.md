[The International workshop on low-frequency oscillations of the Sun and Stars](https://www2.mps.mpg.de/homes/gizon/workshop/index.html) is organized at the Harnack House in Berlin from December 15th to 19th, organized by the Max-Planck Institute.

The workshop holds dedicated sessions related to the ANR-DFG Project Butterfly, joint between Inria Makutu and the University of Göttingen.
